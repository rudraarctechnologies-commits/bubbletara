BubbleTara — Privacy Policy

RudraArc Technologies

Updated 26 October 2026

Who we are

BubbleTara is provided by RudraArc Technologies. This notice explains data use in the current app. Contact us at rudraarctechnologies@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.

Your game data

Levels, scores, coins, rewards, saved boards and settings are stored in the app on your device to run the game and remember progress. We do not operate our own account system, cloud save or analytics service in this version. Optional Google Play Games authentication is described below. The app does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.

Delete BubbleTara data and manage your Play Games profile

BubbleTara is provided by RudraArc Technologies. We do not create a separate developer-operated game account. Optional Google Play Games authentication uses your Google-managed gamer profile.

To remove local BubbleTara levels, coins, saved boards and settings, clear BubbleTara storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. This cannot be restored from a BubbleTara cloud backup; Android backup and device transfer are disabled for game data. This does not delete Google-held data.

To delete BubbleTara data held by Play Games, open your Google Play Games profile, choose Your data, then Delete Play Games account and data, and select BubbleTara under individual game data. You can also delete your entire Play Games profile there; that affects other games and profile information too. Follow Google's current deletion instructions. Play Games data and Google Play History are separate; Google's instructions explain both. Google controls its own retention and deletion processing.

For deletion of feedback or other information sent directly to RudraArc Technologies, email a BubbleTara data deletion request from the address used to contact us. Identify the correspondence to remove; never send a password. We remove support information no longer needed, except information we must retain for applicable obligations. We cannot delete your Google account, Google-held service records, or storage on your phone on your behalf. You do not need to reinstall BubbleTara to use these Google or email request options.

Ads and external services

Core gameplay works offline. Where Google ads are available, Google Mobile Ads services process information such as IP address, approximate location derived from it, ad interactions, diagnostics and device or app identifiers for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Google controls its service data under its privacy policy. This is separate from local game progress. Rewarded ads are optional; availability and daily limits apply.

Feedback and sharing

Email is opened only when you choose it. If you send feedback, we receive your email address and the information you include, and use it to respond and investigate your request. We retain support correspondence only as needed for that purpose and applicable obligations; you can request deletion by email. Your email provider also handles the message under its own terms. Sharing opens an Android chooser; your selected app handles delivery. App sharing sends only a Play Store link; your saved progress is not shared.

Security and updates

Local data is kept in Android app storage. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. This notice will be updated when data practices change. If you are a child, ask a parent or guardian before sending personal information. Contact us about any personal information submitted in error.

Versions 1.0.2 and later: no age popup

Versions 1.0.2 and later do not ask for or use an age group. Every player receives the same child-protective ad settings. Google demo native ads may appear on Home, banners on Levels, and occasional interstitials when proceeding to the next level. Rewarded ads are optional and award coins only after the SDK confirms completion, subject to the displayed daily limit. Closing without completion does not award coins. There are no real-money purchases. All ad requests use child treatment, a general-audience content rating and disabled personalization. The SDK is configured to suppress advertising-ID transmission. The advertising-ID permission remains declared; it does not override child treatment. Privacy initialization uses under-age treatment without asking the player for consent. If privacy checks or connectivity fail, ads stay unavailable and free gameplay continues. Earlier locally saved age answers are removed during migration. Test ads contact Google services and do not generate publisher revenue.

Earlier versions 1.0.0 and 1.0.1

These earlier builds use test ads only and have no real-money purchases. Their closed-test release stores an age group on this device. For users under 16 or with no age answer, it does not initialize advertising or consent services or request ads. Users aged 16 or older can receive Google demo ads only after the privacy checks succeed. Consent choices can be reviewed in Settings when required. The internal developer preview uses Google demo ads. Test ads still contact Google services and are not an offline feature. The closed-test release declares the Android advertising ID permission. For eligible older users, Google Mobile Ads may use the advertising ID, when available, for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Google Privacy Policy

Version 1.0.4: Google Play Games

The version 1.0.4 testing build includes Google Play Games Services for optional platform authentication. Google may automatically connect an existing Play Games profile according to your Google settings; automatic profile-creation prompts are suppressed. You can choose Connect in Settings, or play the entire game without signing in. Google processes account, gamer-profile, app, device and diagnostic information under its own privacy policy. BubbleTara checks authentication status but does not store your Google email, player ID or profile, and does not send local scores, coins, achievements or saved boards to Play Games in this version. Cloud save, online leaderboards and synced achievements are not yet implemented. Manage Play Games sign-in and associated service data through your Google/Play Games settings. Clearing BubbleTara storage removes local progress, but does not delete your Google account or Google-held service data.
